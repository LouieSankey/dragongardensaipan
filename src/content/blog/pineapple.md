# Pineapple: Premium Fruit Economics

Dragon Garden's **pineapple operation** focuses on **premium varieties** - **Rota Red** and **Hawaiian** - that command **$4-6 per fruit** despite requiring **2 years per plant** and **1 fruit per plant**. The secret lies in exceptional quality and unique propagation potential.

## Premium Varieties

### Rota Red Pineapple

**Rare island specialty:**

- **Deep red flesh** with exceptional sweetness
- **Unique to Rota** making it extremely rare
- **Premium market value** due to scarcity
- **Exceptional flavor** justifying high price

### Hawaiian Pineapple

**Classic tropical excellence:**

- **Traditional Hawaiian genetics** with proven quality
- **Balanced sweetness** and acidity
- **Reliable production** in tropical conditions
- **Market recognition** for quality

## The Economics

**High-value, slow-return agriculture:**

- **$4-6 per fruit** premium pricing
- **2-year growing cycle** from plant to harvest
- **1 fruit per plant** making each one precious
- **Quality over quantity** approach

## Propagation Magic

**Each fruit creates an empire:**

- **12+ "pups"** produced per fruit
- **Natural multiplication** expanding the plantation
- **Exponential growth** potential over time
- **Self-sustaining** operation through propagation

## The 200-Plant Vision

**Scaling for weekly harvests:**

- **Current small operation** proving viability
- **200+ plant goal** for consistent production
- **Weekly harvest** potential with proper staggering
- **Sustainable expansion** through pup propagation

## Cultivation Requirements

**Intensive but rewarding:**

- **Well-draining soil** essential for root health
- **Full sun exposure** for optimal fruit development
- **Regular fertilization** supporting slow growth
- **Patience required** for 2-year cycle

## Market Position

**Premium positioning strategy:**

- **Quality over quantity** focus
- **Rare variety** advantage
- **Local market** appreciation for premium fruit
- **High-value crop** maximizing limited space

## Future Potential

**Long-term plantation development:**

- **Gradual expansion** through pup propagation
- **Staggered planting** for continuous harvest
- **Premium market** development
- **Educational value** in high-value agriculture
