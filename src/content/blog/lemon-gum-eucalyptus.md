# Lemon Gum Eucalyptus: Meet Sheila and Her Family

Dragon Garden's **6-7 Lemon Gum Eucalyptus trees** have become towering landmarks, reaching **60+ feet tall** with potential to grow to **150 feet**. Our prized tree **"Sheila"** leads the family as our premier **lemon gum rosin producer**.

## Meet the Family

**Our named trees with distinct personalities:**

- **"Sheila"** - Our star rosin producer
- **6 additional trees** forming an impressive grove
- **Individual characteristics** making each tree unique
- **Collective canopy** creating significant shade and windbreak

## Impressive Scale

**These giants are just getting started:**

- **Current height: 60+ feet** and still growing
- **Potential height: 150 feet** at maturity
- **Massive trunk development** supporting enormous canopy
- **Extensive root systems** anchoring these giants

## Citronella Oil Production

**Natural source of valuable essential oils:**

- **Citronella oil** from leaves and bark
- **Natural insect repellent** properties
- **Aromatherapy applications** for wellness
- **Commercial potential** for essential oil extraction

## Sheila's Special Gift

**Our premium rosin producer:**

- **Lemon gum rosin** harvested from bark
- **Aromatic resin** with multiple applications
- **Traditional uses** in incense and crafts
- **Natural fragrance** with citrus notes

## Environmental Giants

**Massive environmental impact:**

- **Carbon sequestration** on an enormous scale
- **Windbreak protection** for entire garden areas
- **Microclimate creation** through shade and humidity
- **Wildlife habitat** in the towering canopy

## Growth Potential

**Still decades of growth ahead:**

- **Rapid vertical growth** continuing annually
- **Expanding canopy** providing more shade each year
- **Increasing oil production** as trees mature
- **Long-term investment** in natural resources

## Aromatic Experience

**Sensory impact throughout the garden:**

- **Therapeutic environment** created by essential oils
