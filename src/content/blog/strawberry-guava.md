# Strawberry Guava: A Cultivation Adventure in Progress

**We are the only grower of Strawberry Guava on the entire island of Saipan.** Our six precious bushes (5-6 feet tall) represent an ambitious agricultural experiment surrounded by 10-foot tall sword grass.

## What Makes Strawberry Guava Special

- **Intense flavor** combining guava and strawberry notes
- **Small, bite-sized fruits** perfect for snacking
- **High vitamin C content** exceeding most citrus fruits
- **Unique aromatic profile** that's instantly recognizable
- **Beautiful red to yellow skin** when ripe

## The Challenge

Our bushes have shown **occasional flowering** but **haven't fruited yet** - a concerning pattern we're actively investigating:

**Possible causes:**

- **Pollination issues** - lack of appropriate pollinators
- **Environmental stress** from sword grass competition
- **Climate adaptation** period still in progress
- **Microclimate effects** from the unique growing environment

## What We're Doing

- **Soil testing** to ensure optimal nutrients
- **Pollinator observation** and attraction efforts
- **Hand pollination trials** during flowering
- **Microclimate monitoring** within the sword grass environment

## The Promise

Once established, Strawberry Guava offers:

- **Multiple harvests** per year in tropical climates
- **Exceptional flavor** worth the cultivation challenges
- **Unique island delicacy** not available elsewhere
- **Educational value** in agricultural innovation

## Follow Our Journey

This is agricultural pioneering in real-time. While we can't offer tastings yet, you can follow our progress and learn about the challenges of introducing new crops to island environments.
