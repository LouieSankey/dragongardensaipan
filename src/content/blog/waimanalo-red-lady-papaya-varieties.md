# Waimanalo & Red Lady Papayas: Premium Island Varieties

While **local papaya varieties on Saipan are inedible for humans** (though our chickens love them!), our **Waimanalo** and **Red Lady** papayas represent the pinnacle of papaya breeding and flavor.

## The Local Papaya Problem

Native papayas on the island are:

- **Extremely bitter or bland**
- **Poor texture and consistency**
- **Better suited for animal feed**

## Our Premium Varieties

### Waimanalo Papaya (Hawaiian Heritage)

- **Exceptional sweetness** with perfect sugar balance
- **Vibrant orange flesh** that's visually stunning
- **Smooth, buttery texture** that melts in your mouth
- **Classic papaya experience** that converts skeptics

### Red Lady Papaya (Thai Excellence)

- **Deep orange-red flesh** with incredible visual appeal
- **Exceptionally sweet flavor** with complex tropical notes
- **Larger fruit size** than traditional varieties
- **Premium positioning** due to size and appearance

## Why These Varieties Succeed

Our success comes from:

- **Careful variety selection** suited to Saipan's climate
- **Proper soil preparation** and ongoing management
- **Expert harvest timing** for peak ripeness and flavor
- **High nutritional content** - more Vitamin C than oranges!
