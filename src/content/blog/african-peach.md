# African Peach - Ultra-Rare Island First

The moment we've been waiting for has finally arrived - our African Peach trees are showing their first flowers! After investing over 1,000 hours of cultivation effort, both our in-ground tree and 15-gallon container specimen are beginning to bloom.

## Island Exclusive Achievement

African Peach represents the ultimate in rarity for Saipan. This isn't just uncommon - it's completely unknown. Nobody on the island has even heard of African Peach, making this a true botanical first for the Marianas.

## Years of Dedication

Establishing African Peach in the Marianas has been one of our most challenging projects:

- **1,000+ cultivation hours** invested in research and care
- **Multiple growing methods** tested between in-ground and container cultivation
- **First flowering success** on both specimens
- **Breakthrough achievement** for tropical fruit diversity

## Future Expansion Plans

With flowering success confirmed, we're planning ambitious expansion:

- **Clone propagation** from proven parent trees
- **Scale from 8 to 30+ trees** through careful cultivation
- **Genetic preservation** of this ultra-rare variety
- **Island introduction** of completely new fruit genetics

The African Peach flowering marks a historic moment - not just for Dragon Garden, but for tropical fruit cultivation in the entire Marianas region.
