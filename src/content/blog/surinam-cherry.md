# Surinam Cherry: 60+ Bushes Hit Their Stride

Dragon Garden's **massive Surinam Cherry operation** has reached a milestone - **60+ bushes** are experiencing their **first major fruiting year**. What started as experimental cultivation has become our largest berry operation with exciting plans for **Surinam cherry wine**.

**Our largest berry cultivation project:**

- **60+ bushes planted** throughout the property
- **First major fruiting** after years of establishment
- **Largest bush** reaching 8-9 feet tall
