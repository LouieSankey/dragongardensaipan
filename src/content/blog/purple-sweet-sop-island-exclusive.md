# Purple Sweet Sop: An Island Exclusive

We are currently the **only grower of this extraordinary variety on the entire island of Saipan**. The Purple Sweet Sop represents a remarkable variation of the beloved sweet sop with distinctive purple-tinged skin and an unforgettable taste that's been described as **"one of the most delicious fruits you'll ever eat in your life."**

## What Makes It Special

- **Cherry foundation** with **tomato hints** and **bell pepper notes**
- **Creamy, custard-like texture** that melts in your mouth
- **Enhanced sweetness** with subtle floral undertones
- **Larger fruit size** compared to standard sweet sops
- **Complex flavor profile** found nowhere else

## Island Exclusivity

Last year we began sharing this incredible fruit:

- **A dozen fruits** sold to lucky enthusiasts
- **Eight trees** distributed to local growers
- **Only source** currently available on Saipan
- **Future availability** as trees mature in 2-3 years
