# Lemongrass: When Abundance Meets Opportunity

At Dragon Garden Saipan, our **lemongrass** demonstrates the perfect marriage of **abundant growth** and **high-value potential**. What started as aromatic landscaping has evolved into recognition of a significant opportunity - **essential oil extraction** that could transform plentiful tropical growth into liquid gold.

## The Abundance Factor

Our lemongrass showcases explosive growth in Saipan's tropical climate:

- **Dense clumping** that multiplies rapidly
- **Year-round production** of aromatic leaves
- **Self-propagating** through natural division
- **Minimal input** for maximum output
- **Overflow production** beyond immediate garden needs

## The Essential Oil Opportunity

**Lemongrass essential oil represents high-value extraction**:

- **Premium market prices** for quality essential oil
- **Multiple applications** in aromatherapy, cosmetics, and food
- **Growing demand** for natural aromatics
- **Local production** advantage over imported oils

## Applications

- **Aromatherapy** and wellness products
- **Natural cosmetics** and skincare
- **Food flavoring** and beverages
- **Natural cleaning** products and pesticides
- **Traditional medicine** applications

## Cultivation Success

Our lemongrass thrives with:

- **Tropical climate** with consistent warmth
- **Well-draining soil** preventing root rot
- **Full sun exposure** maximizing oil production
- **Minimal maintenance** once established

## Value-Added Agriculture

**From plant to product:**

- **Sustainable harvesting** without plant damage
- **Steam distillation** for essential oil extraction
- **Premium pricing** for quality oils
- **Multiple market channels** for sales
