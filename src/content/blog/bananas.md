# Bananas: Our Tropical Empire

Dragon Garden Saipan boasts an impressive **banana empire** with **50+ stands** spread across our property - **12+ stands** in the main garden area and **30-40 more** in our southern jungle section.

## Our Banana Varieties

### Fiji Apple Bananas

**The crown jewel of our collection:**

- **Ultra-sweet flavor** that's incredibly intense
- **Smaller size** but maximum flavor concentration
- **Apple-like texture** with tropical banana taste
- **Rare variety** not commonly available
- **Premium eating experience** for banana enthusiasts

### Cooking Bananas

**Perfect for culinary applications:**

- **Starchy texture** ideal for cooking methods
- **Versatile preparation** - boiled, fried, baked, or grilled
- **Traditional staple** in tropical cuisines
- **Larger size** than eating bananas
- **Extended harvest** window before full ripeness

### Chiquita Bananas

**Classic variety everyone recognizes:**

- **Familiar flavor** and texture
- **Reliable production** throughout seasons
- **Standard eating** banana experience
- **Good storage** and transport qualities

## Scale of Operation

**Impressive distribution across the property:**

- **12+ established stands** in main garden areas
- **30-40 additional stands** in southern jungle section
- **Multiple varieties** represented throughout
- **Continuous harvests** from different stands
- **Natural succession** of fruiting cycles

## Banana Benefits

- **High potassium** for heart health
- **Natural energy** from healthy carbohydrates
- **Digestive support** through fiber content
- **Vitamin B6** for metabolism
- **Convenient packaging** in nature's wrapper

## Sustainable Cultivation

Our banana stands demonstrate:

- **Low-maintenance** tropical agriculture
- **Natural pest management** through diversity
- **Soil improvement** from organic matter
- **Water efficiency** through natural mulching
- **Continuous production** without replanting
